## The question

Saying that a legal AI tool is “reliable” is not enough: one still needs to know on what, in which jurisdiction, as of which date of the law, and how it was measured. This programme concerns the evaluation of the systems themselves: how to measure their reliability, check their answers and reconstruct the sources and steps that led to them.

## What the programme studies

- **Legal reliability**: measuring the accuracy, relevance and completeness of answers and the quality of the references cited.
- **Traceability**: retrieving the sources and steps needed for a check.
- **Robustness and security**: the effect of adversarial documents, injected instructions and model version changes.
- **Lifecycle governance**: who decides to deploy a system, monitor it, correct it and shut it down.

## Where the programme stands

The programme is planned for the laboratory's second year; its questions are first explored within the first programme, for instance in the approval tests of the AI governance toolkit. No work has been published in it on its own yet.
