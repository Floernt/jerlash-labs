First version of [Glossary of defined terms](/outils/glossaire-definitions/). The tool reads a Word contract and produces its glossary: each defined term, its definition, the article in which it appears and its uses. It flags what is wrong: a repeated or inconsistent definition, a defined term that is never used, capitalised terms without a definition, a spelling that drifts.

It works by rules, without AI, and does not modify the contract. The tool page publishes the measurement made on fictitious contracts it had not seen before.
