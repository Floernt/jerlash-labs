## In brief

The site sets no cookies, does not measure its audience and loads no resources from any other site. Its only script, served by the site itself, draws the decorative backgrounds.

## Visiting the site

The site consists of static pages served by GitHub Pages. According to the GitHub Pages documentation, each visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub processes these logs under its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), which states no retention period specific to GitHub Pages (consulted on 1 November 2026).

The site's publisher has no access to these logs and uses no visitor statistics.

## Your rights

For the connection data kept by GitHub, you may exercise your rights with GitHub, as set out in its privacy statement. You may lodge a complaint with the Autorité de protection des données (Data Protection Authority) ([autoriteprotectiondonnees.be](https://www.autoriteprotectiondonnees.be/)).
