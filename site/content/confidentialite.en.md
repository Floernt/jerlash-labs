## In brief

The site sets no cookies, does not measure its audience and loads no resources from any other site. Its only script, served by the site itself, draws the decorative backgrounds.

## Visiting the site

The site consists of static pages served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical connection data on each visit (IP address, page requested, browser) and may keep it in its logs, in particular for security purposes. ## Contact by email

If you write to the contact address, your message and your email address are kept for as long as necessary to reply and to follow up on any problem reported. They are not used for any other purpose.

## Your rights

You may request access to, rectification or erasure of the data concerning you (emails exchanged) by writing to the contact address. You may lodge a complaint with the Autorité de protection des données (Data Protection Authority) ([autoriteprotectiondonnees.be](https://www.autoriteprotectiondonnees.be/)).
